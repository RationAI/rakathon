01 / UNDERSTAND THE EVIDENCE

One outcome.
Different routes to missing it.

A missed examination can reflect a belief, a difficult experience, an access problem, or an inaccurate record. A non-response alone cannot tell us which. [3][9]

!

Keep the denominators straight.

Czech insurer invitations target women already overdue. Their response rate is not the same as coverage among all eligible women. Screening itself is early detection in people without symptoms. [1][18]

A separate measure: 59.9% coverage. Our calculation from the public Czech dataset for women aged 45–69 in 2024: 1,111,008 examined / 1,856,010 population, using the dataset’s two-year coverage definition. This is not the invitation response rate. [16]

Programme participation, self-reported mammography, and response after a reminder answer different questions.

PROPOSED STUDY DESIGNS

People may have several barriers at once. A trial estimates the effect of the offered intervention; it does not, by itself, prove the psychological mechanism.

03 / A COMMON TESTING STANDARD

Measure visits.
Learn without fooling yourself.

Use one shared protocol so results can be compared. The 90- and 180-day windows below are proposed operational choices, informed by screening trials. [4][10]

POPULATIONVerified overdue

Confirm eligibility and prior mammograms before allocation. Route symptoms to clinical care, not this screening experiment. [18]

ALLOCATIONRandomize 1:1

Assign at woman level; stratify by centre and prior attendance. Keep the control to current standard care. [19]

PRIMARY OUTCOMECompleted by day 90

Claims-confirmed eligible mammography among everyone assigned. Allow claims to mature before the analysis. [10][16]

FOLLOW-UPCheck day 180

Measure later attendance, cost per additional completed screen, complaints, and differences between groups. [4][19]

Decide the question first.

Pre-register the primary outcome, follow-up, minimum worthwhile improvement, sample size, stopping rule and analysis. Report an absolute percentage-point difference and confidence interval, alongside relative effects. Keep women in their assigned group even if they do not read or answer the message. Handle missing outcomes explicitly. [19]

A booking is a step, not the endpoint.

An Australian phone trial found 64.2% booked versus 6.0% without a call, but measured bookings within 14 days. Its effect on completed mammography was not established. Track delivery, contact, booking and attendance separately. [11]

Know what a null result means.

In Singapore, adding rewards, videos or a hotline to mailed and app reminders did not significantly improve overall uptake. A null result is useful; it is not proof that two approaches are equivalent. [13]

Respect the unit of change.

If an intervention changes a whole centre, randomize centres and account for clustering. This requires enough centres and a separate sample-size calculation. Prevent women receiving conflicting variants across overlapping trials. [20]

Assumptions & calculation

Equal independent groups; two-sided test; family-wise α = 0.05; Bonferroni α = 0.05 / planned comparisons. Normal approximation with pooled null variance and unpooled alternative variance; no continuity correction. Rounded up per arm.

n = [z(1−α/2) √(2p̄(1−p̄)) + z(power) √(pA(1−pA)+pB(1−pB))]² / (pB−pA)².

No allowance for missing outcomes, clustering or unequal allocation. Delivery failures remain in intention-to-treat analysis and should already be reflected in the expected control and variant rates. This is a planning aid; finalize the trial with a statistician. [19][26][27]

05 / IMPROVE THE SYSTEM AROUND THE LETTER

Build a pathway
people can use.

These are system recommendations derived from the evidence. Their order is a proposed implementation sequence, not a published ranking of effectiveness.

SUGGESTED ROLLOUT

Start with the parts
you can measure.

  1. FIRSTFix the denominator

    Validate overdue status, contact details, outcome capture and capacity. Interview a sample of non-attendees.

  2. THENRun focused pilots

    Prioritize appointment offers and timed reminders. Test the relevance message separately; preserve balanced information.

  3. AFTER FOLLOW-UPScale selectively

    Use completed visits, uncertainty, cost, patient experience and equity to decide. Check repeat attendance at the next eligible round.

How to read this report

Focused narrative review, not a registered systematic review. Sources were gathered for the Czech, European and global research discussions and checked where needed through 17 October 2026. Data years differ from publication years. Observational associations and stated barriers do not establish causation. Proposed messages, trial designs and rollout priorities are our synthesis, not findings attributed to the cited authors. No private patient records or predictive-model results are used.
