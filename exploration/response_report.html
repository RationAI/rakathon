Regional evaluation of mammography screening response

How women in each kraj and okres respond to the screening invitation, and how that relates to travel distance and waiting time. Modelled on the structure of the NSC regional availability slides, using the Rakhaton 2026 invitation data.

Data

  • Rakhaton 2026: one row per invitation, 2020–2024, with 3, 6 and 12 month screening outcomes.
  • Area is the VZP territorial area, which is an okres (Praha, Brno, Ostrava and Plzeň are single areas).
  • Screening centres, booking waits and opening hours scraped from mamo.cz (snapshot ).

Indicators

  • Response: share of invitations followed by a screening within the window.
  • Distance: straight line from the district point to the nearest centre.
  • Wait: mean booking wait of the three nearest centres, in days.
  • Kraj values combine districts weighted by invitations.

Limits

  • Rates are raw, with no adjustment for age or screening history.
  • Invitations, not the whole population of women aged 45–69, so not the NSC coverage figure.
  • Staffing, equipment and screening volumes per centre are not in this data. Population: ČSÚ women aged 40+, mean 2020–2024. Citizenship is not ethnicity: it covers foreign nationals only and says nothing about self-declared nationality (census). Immigration and citizenship: ČSÚ foreigners (residence permit holders, not asylum holders), 31 Dec 2024; the count of foreign women rose sharply in 2022 with Ukrainian refugees. Invitations come from one insurer (VZP), whose share of the population differs by district, so per-capita values are only comparable as a rough guide.
  • Centres and waits are today's snapshot, not 2020–2024.

Bruntál and Jeseník have no VZP area in the invitation data, so they are grey on invitation-based maps; the distance, waiting-time, immigration and citizenship maps include them (for these two districts, distance and waits are estimated from the district centroid). Kraj values on those maps are weighted by women, not invitations.
