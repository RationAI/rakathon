Who will not come, why, and what to do about it

Task

  • Who: score every invitation by its chance of a screening.
  • Why: the drivers and eight explainable segments.
  • What: one intervention per segment. How to verify: a randomised test.

Evaluation

  • Label: screened within 12 months of the invitation (all numbers on this page).
  • Split by woman, 70 / 15 / 15; numbers are on the held-out test women.
  • ROC AUC: chance that a screened invitation scores above a non-screened one.

Source

  • Models: training/classifiers (explain, ablation), training/per_group_experts.
  • Segments: segmentation/segments.py.
  • Built by build_results.py.

    03 What: an intervention per segment

    Hypotheses to test, not proven effects. The segment says why the woman does not come; the intervention uses the channel she already uses.

    04 How to verify: the experiment

    A randomised test per segment: half of the invitations get the intervention, half the standard letter. The table shows the invitations needed per arm to detect +2 percentage points (two-sided α = 5%, power 80%) and how many a year of invitations provides.

    Design

    1. Assign every new invitation its segment with the rules above (no model needed, explainable to a doctor).
    2. Randomise by woman within each segment: intervention or control (standard letter). Keep the woman in her arm on later invitations.
    3. Measure screening within 3, 6 and 12 months; the primary outcome is 12 months.
    4. Size each arm from the table; segments with too few invitations run longer or pool regions.
    5. Decide per segment: keep, change or drop the intervention; cost per extra screened woman.
    6. Re-score with the model to target the next round within each segment.

    Invitations per arm needed vs. available in a year

    Caveats

    • Observational data: the drivers and segments describe who comes, not what makes her come. Only the experiment shows the effect of an intervention.
    • The 12-month label of invitations from late 2024 may be incomplete; 2020 is lower because of COVID (see exploration).
    • Women aged 40–44 appear only as first invitations, so age and first invitation are confounded.
    • Response by region and district is in the response map and the regional report; location shifts the level, not the drivers.
