    Data quality notes

    Clean

    • No duplicate rows; INVITATION_ID unique.
    • Every missing value is structural: no previous invitation, never screened in 5 years, or not registered with the doctor.
    • PERSON_ID has leading zeros: read it as text.

    Quirks

    • INVITATION_SEQUENCE restarts at 1 after a long break; PRIOR_INVITATION_COUNT is the running total.
    • 52 women start at sequence 2 (an earlier invitation is missing); fixed in the prepared data.
    • 3,354 rows: screened before but count 0 (60-month window edge); 193 rows over 60 months since the last screen.
    • PRIOR_SCREEN_COUNT_5Y is mostly 2: regular two-yearly screening.

    Modelling consequences

    • Most women appear several times: split by woman (70/15/15), never by row.
    • 2020 responds lower (COVID): use 2021–2024 for baseline rates.
    • Base rates are low: pick decision thresholds on validation data, not 0.5.
